What is STEMPunks?

STEMPunks is a school STEM club where students can explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through hands-on projects, experiments, and whatever interesting ideas we come up with.

What We Do

Technology

Programming, electronics, computers, and other questionable things involving electricity.

Science

Experiments, demonstrations, and figuring out why things behave the way they do.

Engineering

Design, build, test, fail, redesign, and eventually produce something that mostly works.

Math

Explore the mathematics behind the things we build and the problems we solve.
